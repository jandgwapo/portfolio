<section class="case-study-block">
  <h2>The Project</h2>
  <div class="case-study-block-body">
    <p>
      PRMS Matalom is a parish records management and information website for St. Joseph Parish in Matalom, Leyte. The
      goal was to bring public information and useful records workflows into one clearer digital experience.
    </p>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="case-study-block">
  <h2>The Build</h2>
  <div class="case-study-block-body">
    <p>
      I worked across the stack, from responsive screens and form flows to server-side logic and database-backed
      content. The implementation uses Vanilla PHP, CSS, and MySQL, keeping the interface practical for desktop and
      mobile users.
    </p>
    <p>
      The most important part of the work was making the system easy to understand: clear navigation, focused forms,
      useful feedback, and a structure that can grow as the parish adds more information.
    </p>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="case-study-block">
  <h2>Outcome</h2>
  <div class="case-study-block-body">
    <ul>
      <li>A live, responsive parish website and records workflow.</li>
      <li>Vanilla PHP and MySQL foundations for structured information.</li>
      <li>CSS components that keep pages consistent.</li>
      <li>A simpler experience for visitors and staff using the system.</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
</section>
